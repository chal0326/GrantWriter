# Grant Writing Knowledge Base

## Primary Funding Sources
- **NEA (National Endowment for the Arts):** [arts.gov](https://www.arts.gov)
- **NYFA (New York Foundation for the Arts):** [nyfa.org](https://www.nyfa.org)
- **Creative Capital:** [creative-capital.org](https://creative-capital.org)

## Functional Requirements (The 10-Step Protocol)
1. **Research:** Use Tavily to scrape NYFA/NEA portals weekly.
2. **Interpret:** Feed PDF guidelines to Claude to extract `Eligibility` and `Hard Deadlines`.
3. **Narratives:** Use the "Impact-Vision-Execution" framework.
4. **Budgets:** Standardize labor rates based on W.A.G.E. standards.
5. **Supporting Materials:** Auto-format Resumes/CVs into grantor-preferred styles.
6. **Alignment:** Reframe project tags (e.g., "Street Art" -> "Urban Community Engagement").
7. **Tracking:** Use a Kanban-style database for status (Researching, Drafting, Submitted).
8. **Refinement:** Apply a "Clarity & Fluff" filter to all drafts.
9. **Coordination:** Generate interview questions for the artist to fill in narrative gaps.
10. **Reporting:** Generate post-award templates for financial reporting.

## Key APIs & Tools
- **Anthropic Claude API:** For narrative generation and guideline interpretation.
- **Google Drive API:** To store/retrieve artist portfolios and work samples.
- **SendGrid:** For automated deadline notifications.
