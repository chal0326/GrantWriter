MARIA ELENA VÁSQUEZ
Painter & Community Muralist
New York, NY | mariavart@email.com

STATEMENT
My work investigates the intersection of memory, diaspora, and urban identity through large-scale
murals and oil painting. I am committed to community engagement and collaborative creation with
underserved youth in under-resourced neighborhoods.
